# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Bearden High School students (grades 9–12) deciding whether to join the robotics club, often browsing on a phone or a school Chromebook.
- Parents checking what the club is and how their student gets involved.
- Local businesses and community members considering sponsoring the club.

## Product Purpose

The public website for the Bearden High School Robotics Club (Knoxville, TN; Bearden Bulldogs). It explains what the club does, gets students to join, and gives sponsors and parents a way to make contact. Success means a student who lands on the site knows how to join and a sponsor knows who to contact.

## Positioning

A school club that builds with VEX robotics hardware for the craft of building, not for a competition calendar. The club does not currently compete in any league or tournament.

## Operating Context

- The club builds robots with VEX parts (metal structure, motors, wheels, brain, controller).
- The site is a static Vite + React + framer-motion single page, hosted on Railway and deployed from `main`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections in v1: Hero, About, Join, Sponsors, Contact.
- No competition results, rankings, or team numbers exist; never imply them.
- Undecided / not yet provided (ship as visibly marked placeholders): meeting days, times and room; faculty advisor name; contact email; sponsor names and logos; member counts; photos of the club and its robots.

## Brand Commitments

- School colors: maroon and gray (Bearden Bulldogs).
- Name: "Bearden High Robotics Club" / "Bearden Robotics".

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no photos, logos, sponsors, testimonials, or stats. Do not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Make joining obvious: one clear way for a student to get involved.
2. Show building, not trophies: the club's identity is making robots.
3. Honest placeholders over invented facts.
4. Works well on a phone and a low-power school Chromebook.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes teenagers and parents on varied devices. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, and full respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
